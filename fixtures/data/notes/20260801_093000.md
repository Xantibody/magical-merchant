# Where the list pane should live

The list should float over the body while the pointer is on the rail, and
stay out of the way otherwise. Pushing the body sideways moves the line
you are writing, which is the one thing that must not move.

Two open questions:

- what happens on a narrow window, where there is no room to float
- whether the pin belongs in the pane or on the rail
